About Me

I am a Master’s graduate in Mathematics from the Central University of Kashmir. My research interests cover abstract algebra, semigroup theory, and their interactions with topology, geometry, and theoretical computer science. My master’s dissertation, titled “Ranks and Presentations for Finite Transformation Semigroups”, sparked a lasting interest in the structural study of algebraic objects.
